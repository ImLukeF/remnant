Private development experiment, 17 November 2026.

A temporal convolutional encoder plus past-only GRU was tested on three fixed participant folds across all 84 previously observed people. None of its four configurations improved both paired mean macro F1 and balanced accuracy. The best balanced-accuracy configuration reached 41.79%, versus the existing tree method at 42.64%; its macro F1 was 0.3119 versus 0.3062. No fresh test cohort was prepared, no weights were promoted, and model-driven waking remains disabled.

The verified detailed cache contains 169 recording blocks and 18,858 usable 30-second intervals (157.15 hours). Each interval contains 240 frames of 24 relative Mel bands and band power. Every previous summary observation was independently reproduced before accepting its detailed frames. Dataset revision, source hashes, licence evidence, extraction fingerprint and derived-file hashes are archived here. Raw recordings were removed after extraction; raw audio and clinical epoch arrays are excluded from this repository.

protocol.json and candidates.json retain the training design and full fold results. training-job.json and data-job.json record exact-source VPS receipts. verification/ and native-verification.json cover independent Python/Swift math, malformed imports, streaming feature parity and an iOS audio-target cross-compile. These focused checks establish implementation agreement, not phone sleep accuracy, overnight battery use or TV tolerance. Both repositories remain private.
