Private development experiment, 17 November 2026.

All 84 previously observed clinical participants were development data, divided into three fixed participant folds (31 / 25 / 28). These results are model-selection estimates, not a fresh independent test. The paired tree baseline averaged macro F1 0.3062 and balanced accuracy 42.64%.

Every candidate failed to improve both paired metrics. No new final cohort was prepared or scored, and no candidate was promoted to Parley. The initial recurrent experiment wrote a provisional selected-model file before the stricter admission rule was added; that model is rejected and deliberately omitted here.

protocol.json records source, fitting settings, feature-cache checksums and participant assignments. candidates.json retains fold metrics and fitting histories; job.json proves the exact-source VPS run. Only derived reports are archived. Raw audio, clinical epoch arrays and phone measurements are excluded.
